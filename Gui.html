GUI

Gui Guilherme Carvalho dos Santos (born 22 June 2002) is a Brazilian professional basketball player for the Golden State Warriors of the National Basketball Association (NBA). Santos made his professional debut with Minas of the Novo Basquete Brasil (NBB) during the 2018–19 season.[2] He averaged 6.7 points, 5.7 rebounds, 2.5 assists, 10.5 efficiency and 23.0 minutes on the court - at only 16 years of age.[3] In the 2018–19 NBB season, because Santos was still in school and for several other reasons, he was unable to fully follow the adult team's routine.[4
