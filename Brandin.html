BRANDIN

Brandin Brandin Thomas Podziemski is an American professional basketball guard for the Golden State Warriors of the NBA. A 2023 first-round pick from Santa Clara, he earned NBA All-Rookie First Team honors in 2024 and WCC Co-Player of the Year in college. Known for his scoring, rebounding, and playmaking, Podziemski has set multiple career-highs, including 30 points in 2026, and represented the USA Select Team.
